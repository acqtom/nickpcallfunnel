Do Any Of These Sound Familiar?

These are the gaps that stop capable investors from ever getting a development done.

1

You Don't Know How To Deploy Your Capital

You've got it sitting there ready. But nobody's told you the order things happen in. Do you buy the land in cash or finance it? When do you hire the build team? How much stays in the bank versus goes into the deal? Money isn't the problem, knowing how to use it is.

2

You Don't Know How To
Vet Good Contractors

You've never hired anyone for a build, so you can't tell the difference between someone reliable and someone telling you what you want to hear to win the contract. You don't know what to ask, or what a fair number looks like for the work. So you'd be picking on gut feel.

3

You Don't Know How To Manage
A Construction Project

You've never run a team of tradesmen. So you don't know what should be happening on site each week, who has to finish before who starts, or what it looks like when something's going wrong. And every week it slips, you're paying for it.

4

You Don't Know How To Get
Plans And Permits Approved

You've never submitted anything to a city. So you don't know what drawings they want, who stamps them, or how many rounds of back and forth it takes. You don't even know what you're allowed to build on a lot before you buy it.

5

You Don't Know How To
Find Deals Worth Buying

Every lot you look at is on the public market, already priced for somebody else's profit. You know the good ones get bought before they ever show up, you just don't know who's getting them or how. And when you do find one, you can't tell if it's real.

6

You Don't Know How To
Underwrite A Development Deal

You can put a spreadsheet together. What you can't do is tell whether the numbers in it are the right ones. You don't know a realistic build cost, what soft costs you're missing, or what it actually sells for when it's done. The math will be perfect and still be wrong.

The 3-Part Framework Behind
Every Student Breakthrough

Every real estate developer who comes through Investor's Blueprint gets a fully customized roadmap built around these three systems.

Source System

Two things here. First, I plug you into my network of off-market deals, the good ones that never make it to the public market. But more importantly, I teach you how to find and check deals on your own, so you can spot a good one from a bad one before you ever commit a dollar. So whether it's a deal I point you to or one you find yourself, you'll know how to run the numbers and tell if it's actually worth doing.

Capital System

I hand you my playbook for raising money from real investors: how to find them, how to present the deal so they say yes, and how to fund projects far bigger than your own savings could. So you stop walking away from good deals just because you don't have all the cash. Once you can raise money, you can take on deals many times bigger than anything you could do alone.

Build System

Two things here as well. First, I give you access to my vetted team, my contractors and lenders, the network it took me a decade to build. And second, whether you use my people or your own, I show you exactly how to run the build the right way, from an empty lot all the way to sold. So you're not just avoiding the mistakes that eat your profit, you're building smart enough to actually make money on the deal, even if it's your first one.
